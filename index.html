Environmental Analysis

Critical Hazards & Engineering Defenses

Hazard Condition 01 ℹ HOVER FOR DEEP-DIVE

Total Darkness

The crater floor has received zero sunlight for over two billion years. Artemis-7 has no solar generation and operates on a dying battery clock with rapid cryogenic cooldown. Conventional optical cameras and stereo vision fail completely in absolute cryogenic pitch-black.

Defeated By RRU-1 Architecture
  • Wired Umbilical Tether: Continuous rim-generated 400V power eliminates battery clock dependencies entirely.
  • 12,000-Lumen Floodlight Array: High-output dual-frequency LED searchlight illuminates the capture cone on demand.
  • Signal-Based Locating: Autonomous radio frequency triangulation locates the asset without requiring optical illumination.
AEROSPACE THREAT DOSSIER // TOTAL DARKNESS
PHYSICS OF OPERATING WITHOUT LIGHT

In Shackleton's Permanently Shadowed Region (PSR), solar irradiance is strictly 0.0 W/m² and ambient temperature plummets to 40 K (-233°C). Radiative cooling freezes fluorosilicone lubricants solid, while lithium batteries suffer electrolyte freezing within minutes. Optical cameras encounter zero usable photons (0.000 lux), causing visual odometry, feature tracking, and stereoscopic depth calculation to fail completely.

ENGINEERING DEFENSE IN-DEPTH
WIRED TETHER: Delivers 400V AC power from rim fuel cells, supplying continuous heater power (>180 K) to avoid cryogenic cold-welding and eliminating the ticking battery clock.
12,000-LM FLOODLIGHT: High-CRI LED matrix with focused 15° beam casts 450 lux across 90m, cutting through extreme shadow contrast to reveal sample bays.
SIGNAL LOCATING: 437 MHz UHF interrogator and S-band beacon homing propagate through vacuum unhindered by optical darkness or electrostatic dust.
Hazard Condition 02 ℹ HOVER FOR DEEP-DIVE

Steep Slopes

The 32° slope is dangerously close to the ~35° angle of repose of loose, uncompacted lunar regolith. Any rescue vehicle attempting descent would disturb the shear plane, triggering an unstoppable regolith avalanche that slides both vehicles into the crater floor.

Defeated By RRU-1 Architecture
  • Rim Reach Geometry: The heavy recovery chassis never crosses the rim edge; all forces operate from stable bedrock.
  • Mechanical Bedrock Anchoring: Twin hydraulic rotary anchors pin the rover chassis to basalt rock before boom articulation.
  • Self-Locking Worm-Gear Winch: Irreversible mechanical drive holds cable tension passively, even during sudden power cutoff.
GEOTECHNICAL HAZARD DOSSIER // STEEP SLOPES
PHYSICS OF THE ANGLE OF REPOSE

Unconsolidated lunar regolith has an internal friction angle φ ≈ 35°–38°. Under Coulomb shear failure (τ = c + σ·tan φ), vacuum cohesion c is near zero in the top 30cm layer. At 32°, the slope factor of safety is FS = tan(35°)/tan(32°) ≈ 1.12—a precarious 12% margin. Any wheel track shear instantaneously fluidizes the upper granular bed into an unstoppable powder avalanche that would bury Artemis-7.

ENGINEERING DEFENSE IN-DEPTH
RIM REACH GEOMETRY: The 320kg RRU-1 vehicle remains stationed on consolidated rim basalt, imposing exactly 0 Pa of surcharge pressure onto the fragile shear slope.
ROTARY ANCHORING: Twin tungsten-carbide augers penetrate 65cm into basalt bedrock with >1,200 N tension hold, counter-acting 2,400 N·m of boom overturning moment.
WORM-GEAR WINCH: Lead angle γ = 4.2° < arctan(μ_s) creates a physically irreversible lock. Gravity cannot back-drive the drum even under total electrical cutoff.

Deterministic Control

Safety Interlocks & Fail-Safe Architecture

Proof of intentional, controlled operation. Every mechanical transition requires affirmative hardware sensor validation before execution.

Philosophy: Intentional, Controlled Operation
On an unstable 32° cryogenic regolith slope, there are no second chances. Software cannot override physical constraints. Every actuation is guarded by hardwired electrical relays and sensor interlocks, ensuring that human hesitation, communication blackouts, or computer faults cannot cause runaway motion or drop the irreplaceable water-ice core.
INTERLOCK 01 MOBILITY SAFETY
Drive stops on dead-man release
The traction drive circuit requires continuous active operator signal hold. If the dead-man switch is released or remote link heartbeats lapse for >250ms, power is instantly cut and spring-actuated electromagnetic brakes lock all 4 wheels.
FAIL-SAFE ACTION:
SPRING DISC BRAKE LOCKED
INTERLOCK 02 RIGGING STRUCTURAL
Boom extension requires anchor lock
The articulating boom cannot rotate out over the crater lip until dual hydraulic bedrock anchors confirm >800 N pre-load hold. The boom extension motor is physically de-energized by hardware relays until both anchor switches close.
FAIL-SAFE ACTION:
BOOM HARD-LOCKED AT DECK
INTERLOCK 03 PAYLOAD TRAJECTORY
No winch-down until position fixed
The winch drum is prevented from spooling down into the abyss until a confirmed coordinate fix (Wake ACK, RF Triangulation, or Optical laser scan) is verified and locked in flight computer memory. Blind cable lowering is electrically blocked.
FAIL-SAFE ACTION:
WINCH DESCENT POWER CUT
INTERLOCK 04 CAPTURE VERIFICATION
No winch-up until payload switch detects cache
The hoist motor cannot run in reverse to raise the scoop cup until the internal mechanical microswitch and dual break-beam sensors affirmatively confirm the 2.4kg ice canister is seated squarely inside the cup.
FAIL-SAFE ACTION:
HOIST MOTOR INTERLOCKED
INTERLOCK 05 TRANSIT ISOLATION
No driving until lid closed and stowed
The chassis traction motors are locked out whenever the boom is cantilevered or the scoop cup is open. Traction can only be re-energized once the scoop lid is mechanically clamped and the boom is locked in its deck travel cradle.
FAIL-SAFE ACTION:
WHEEL MOTORS DISABLED
INTERLOCK 06 PASSIVE PHYSICS
Power-off = winch holds
The winch relies on an irreversible worm-gear gear train with lead angle below friction coefficient (μ > tan γ). If all electrical power is lost, severed, or turned off, the mechanical gears physically cannot back-drive. Gravity cannot drop the payload.
FAIL-SAFE ACTION:
PHYSICAL WORM GEAR LOCK
